About

~/hisham/README.md

I'm a software engineer in Amman who likes building things people use every day.

At Ejada I work on digital banking apps across web and mobile: React and React Native on the front end, ASP.NET Core APIs behind them, real-time sync over WebSockets, fraud-detection modules, and CI/CD pipelines in Azure DevOps and GitHub Actions.

In 2026 I completed SAP's Young Professionals Program, where I worked through end-to-end business processes in SAP Business Suite and built with SAP's Generative AI Hub. That pushed me toward the mix I enjoy most: solid full-stack engineering with AI and cloud built in. I'm now working through the AWS AI Academy.

Outside work I ship side projects: an AI development studio with a voice assistant, a vector design app for sign makers, and full-stack platforms in TypeScript and PostgreSQL.
